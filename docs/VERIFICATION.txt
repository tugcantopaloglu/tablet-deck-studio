REVIEW AND LIMITS
- Independent code/UX review scored the stale-response and keyboard-focus findings resolved. Documentation records inherited Studio tokens and the scoped extension; no new visual world was introduced.
- No browser, CUA, or app window was opened during development. Rendered layout, physical tablet touch, assistive-technology behavior, and live Soundpad favourite add/play still need rehearsal. Earlier browser layout checks are not counted as rerun.

- Backups keep 20 valid snapshots of board configuration/favourites and reference external files; they do not copy sound files/apps or Soundpad's native library.
- Previous physical USB/Wi-Fi, real OBS scenes/mute, audible Soundpad playback, and user-confirmed Discord local microphone-test results are documented separately in LIVE-TEST.md. Remote Discord listener audio, live broadcast/record/replay, physical cable/reconnect stability, and Voicemeeter hardware remain unverified.
